About Kickenut and how to use it

Kickenut helps you find official cancellation links when you want to cancel a subscription or unsubscribe from a service. Enter a company name to look for a route to its cancellation page.

Kickenut finds the link. You complete the cancellation with your subscription provider. Searching or opening a result does not automatically cancel anything or stop a payment.

How to unsubscribe from a service

  1. Enter the company. On Kickenut’s search page, type the name of the company or subscription you want to cancel.
  2. Choose Search. Wait for Kickenut to check for an official cancellation link.
  3. Open the result. On a phone, choose Unsubscribe now. On desktop, choose Open official result. These buttons open the provider’s page.
  4. Complete the provider’s steps. Sign in if required, follow the cancellation instructions and check for confirmation from the provider.

If the cancellation link doesn’t help

Return to Kickenut and choose Official Website when it is available. Sign in on the provider’s website and look for its subscription, account or billing settings.

Some searches may not find a verified cancellation link or official website. Check the company name and try again. If you still need help, contact the provider through its official support channels.

Cancel subscriptions and manage recurring payments

If you are looking to cancel a subscription, end a membership or stop a recurring subscription payment, Kickenut helps you find where to begin. The provider controls its cancellation process, any notice period and whether a refund is available.

Keep the provider’s cancellation confirmation. Use Kickenut’s search again whenever you want to look up another subscription.
